# From [duck.ai](duck.ai)
## Recommended ingredients (for oily, acne-prone, matte-finish skin)
- **Glycerin / Hyaluronic acid / Sodium hyaluronate** — humectants that draw and hold water without adding oil; keep skin hydrated so sebum production balances.
- **Niacinamide** — reduces sebum production, improves barrier function, calms inflammation, and helps fade post‑inflammatory hyperpigmentation.
- **Salicylic acid (BHA)** — oil‑soluble exfoliant that penetrates pores to reduce comedones and unclog follicles.
- **Ceramides / Panthenol (pro‑vitamin B5) / Cholesterol** — barrier‑repair lipids that restore skin integrity and reduce irritation from actives.
- **Lightweight emollients (squalane, caprylic/capric triglyceride, jojoba esters)** — provide non‑greasy slip and moisturization without clogging pores.
- **Zinc PCA** — helps control sebum and has antimicrobial/anti‑inflammatory benefits.
- **Dimethicone / silica / mattifying powders** — create a smooth, non‑shiny finish and reduce surface oil.
- **Azelaic acid** — reduces inflammation, fights acne bacteria, and lightens pigmentation with generally good tolerability.

## Ingredients to avoid (or use with caution)
- **Coconut oil / Cocoa butter / High-oleic botanical oils** — highly comedogenic for many; can clog pores and worsen acne.
- **Mineral oil (if you react to occlusives)** — can feel heavy/occlusive; some oily-skin users find it promotes congestion.
- **Heavy butters (shea butter, mango butter)** — nourishing but often too occlusive for oily/acne-prone skin.
- **Alcohol denat / Isopropyl alcohol (frequent use)** — may feel mattifying short-term but strips skin, increasing rebound oil and irritation.
- **Fragrance / Essential oils** — common irritants and sensitizers that can cause inflammation and breakouts.
- **High concentrations of irritating AHA/BHA with no buffering** — over‑exfoliation damages barrier and worsens acne; use appropriate pH/concentration.
- **Comedogenic esters/oils (isopropyl myristate, lauric acid in some formulas)** — can block pores for acne-prone users.
- **Occlusive-heavy formulas (thick balms/ointments) for daytime** — can trap sebum and bacteria; better for night only if needed.

## Quick shopping tips
- Look for labels: **"oil-free"**, **"non-comedogenic"**, **"gel-cream"**, **"mattifying"**.
- Prefer products with humectants + lightweight emollients + barrier lipids (ceramides/panthenol) rather than heavy occlusives.
- Patch-test new products and introduce one product at a time.
